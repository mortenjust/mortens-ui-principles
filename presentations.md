1. Presentations are stories with the audience as the hero. You are not the hero. The product is not the hero. You are the hero's mentor. 
2. Use comparisons generously. Side by side. Screenshots. Data. Always side by side. Call out contrasts.
3. If you are presenting ideas, compare what is and what could be
4. Use fewer words than you want

6. When presenting products, ui, design, screens, always follow the user. Everything should be phrased in terms of what the user experiences

# Hero's journey
1. Intro. Ordinary world. likable/flawed hero.
2. call to adventure
3. reluctant, maybe refuse call
4. encouraged by mentor
5. cross the first threshold to special world
6. encounter tests, allies and enemies
7. inmost cave
8. endure the ordeal
9. possession of reward
10. pursued on the road back to ordinary world
11. resurrection and transformation
12. return with elexir, treasure, benefit

# Acts
Act 1. set up story, characters, relationships, establish hero's unfullfilled desire (plot)
Act 2. hero meets obstacles
act 3. resolve 

Follow the above but avoid doing anything outright barbaric or forced.
